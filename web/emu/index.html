Stems: a backup's song or section as separate WAV tracks (ZIP)

Back up the FM-1 from the editor or the installer ("Back up": a JIANT-backup-….json file), then load it here. PICTURE
UMBRA LIVE · GROUND PLATE (on the FM-1: SELECT in TOTALITY)
HOME: WELCOME, then UMBRA (LIVE AUDIO CTRLS, LIVE VIZ CTRLS, BEING, ROUTES), then GEN. The picture plays behind WELCOME and the CTRLS pages. WELCOME is six pages: OCT+ the next, OCT− the one before; OCT− held: after a second a count from 5, and at its end WELCOME is gone (let go before: it stays; MENU › SYSTEM › WELCOME ON brings it back). Twelve plates: CORONA is built in; the other eleven come with an install, in a bank of 16 the plates page can add to (in the emulator all are built in). BEACON, ZODIAC, SONAR, MOTHS, MARSH and PACHINKO are line art from UMBRA OBSCURA, the game.
LIVE AUDIO CTRLS: one track at a time (ALGORITHM): its four slots of LFO › MOD's matrix; KNOB 1 SLOT, 2 SOURCE, 3 DEST, 4 AMOUNT; under them what each macro moves. LIVE VIZ CTRLS: a page at a time (ALGORITHM): every plate, then BODIES, SCREEN, EFFECTS and BUTTONS. Left: the page's name and its elements (KNOB 1), each with a bar that moves as its controls move it; under a plate's eight, its other elements (each one's name and ON / OFF, its parameters set in under it), set, linked and kept in presets like the eight. Right: the chosen row's controls (KNOB 2): on the name's row its KIND (GROUND or VEIL) and PRESET, and DICE (KNOB 2: each turn a new throw of the other elements, in the preset chosen; a TIMELINE row picks a preset, so rows can each have their own); on an element SET (KNOB 4 its value), NOTES (KNOB 3 a track, KNOB 4 on or off: the tracks whose notes drive it; none: AUTO, the plate's own choice), its links (KNOB 3 the source: KNOB 1–4, a track's HIT, LFO or STEP, LEVEL, BEAT, the five buttons' TAPs; OFF removes it; KNOB 4 how far, −99 to 99) and + ADD (KNOB 3: a new link). OCT+ adds a link to the chosen element (on LIVE AUDIO CTRLS a slot), OCT− removes the chosen one. At first KNOB 1–3 move a plate's first three elements, KNOB 4 takes LIGHT down, LFO TAP inverts, ENV TAP glitches, EDIT TAP is MOSAIC.
Plates are grounds (CINDER, GRANULE, CORONA, TARN, SEPIA, GYRE, AURORA, FATHOM, RAPIDS), which lie only on MAIN, or VEILS (BEACON, ZODIAC, SONAR, MOTHS, MARSH, PACHINKO, KOI, MEDUSA, and BODIES, UMBRA's figures), which lie only on OVER and LAYER 3. A ground of OFF is black, so veils can stand alone. A veil that does not fit in memory beside a large ground waits until it does.
TOTALITY: HOME on HOME (or PLAY on SAVE › TIMELINE / ARRANGE). PRESETS the ground (the old one fades out, the new one in), SELECT the OVER veil; while the song plays, a plate or layer changed here takes the picture over (GLO lit: the rows let it be), and GLO tapped hands it back at the next row; KNOB 1–4 the macros M1–M4, sent on as the CTRLS pages say; FX tapped: KNOB 1–4 MIRROR MOSAIC GLITCH SOLAR; SEL, ENV, ARP tapped: LAYER 3, OVER, MAIN hidden or back (half a second's fade; LAYER 3 empty: BODIES laid on it); held with ALGORITHM turned: the next plate on that layer; LFO tapped the strobe, EDIT the blocks (LIVE VIZ CTRLS › BUTTONS reassigns all five); HOME back.
SCREEN's elements: MOVE where a track's notes land: STATIC (by pitch, as ever), MOVE (a step along the track's own path each note), DRIFT (a step as long as the time since its last note), RANDOM; TRAVEL how far; SPEED; REACT; DISSLV (how long TOTALITY's fade between grounds takes).
Projector: TOTALITY as large as a window, following the FM-1 over USB (older firmware: a plate played by its notes, MIDI OUT: ALL).
